可以。既然目标是**一天集中记完**，就不再拆成多天，而是把这些词尽可能塞进几个连续、夸张、有画面的语境里。

关键不是今天把每个词的所有含义都背下来，而是做到：

**看到/听到英文 → 马上想起场景 → 马上理解意思。**

下面我把你的词尽量按照主题串起来。基础词也放进故事里，但你不用刻意背它们。

## 场景一：买房，却差点遇到骗局

想象你今天准备买人生第一套豪宅。

> After getting **married**, Robin wanted to buy a luxurious **house**.
> But taking out a **mortgage** would place a significant financial **burden** on the family.
>
> A **broker** told Robin about an unbelievable offer.
> The company claimed it could **incorporate** all the fees into the mortgage.
>
> The offer **sounded** perfect.
>
> But there was a serious **flaw**.
>
> The entire business was **fraud**.
>
> The scammers had deliberately **targeted** vulnerable home buyers and **lulled** them into a false sense of security.
>
> Robin was **confronted** with a choice: lose the family's **savings**, or report the **brokers** to law **enforcement**.

### 这一幕只记一个画面：

🏠 → 💰 → 😈 → 🚨

房子 → mortgage → broker → fraud → scammers → savings → enforcement

核心词：

**mortgage** = 房贷
→ take out a mortgage

**burden** = 负担
→ financial burden

**broker** = 经纪人、中间商
→ mortgage broker / data broker

**incorporate** = 纳入、合并
→ incorporate A into B

**fraud** = 欺诈
→ credit-card fraud

**flaw** = 缺陷
→ a serious flaw

**flawed** = 有缺陷的
→ a flawed system

**targeted** = 有针对性的
→ targeted advertising

**vulnerable** = 易受伤害的
→ vulnerable to fraud

**lull** = 使放松警惕
→ lull someone into...

**confront** = 面对
→ be confronted with

**savings** = 积蓄
→ life savings

**enforcement** = 执法
→ law enforcement

# 场景二：你买完房，发现智能家居正在监视你

这是你这批词中最值得记的一组。

想象晚上回到新家。

灯自动亮了。

智能音箱突然说：

> “Hello.”

但你根本没有叫它。

然后你发现……

> The smart-home system had been **monitoring** the family.
>
> It **monitored** their **browsing** activity, gathered information about their shopping habits, and even recorded **intimate** details about their lives.
>
> Nobody had **consented** to this level of monitoring.
>
> The company was actively **processing** the information and distributing it to dozens of **affiliates** and data **brokers**.
>
> The information was then used for **targeted** advertising and **sponsored** content.

画面：

👀 **monitoring**

→ 🌐 **browsing**

→ 🗃️ **gathered**

→ ❤️ **intimate**

→ ❌ **consented**

→ ⚙️ **processing**

→ 📤 **distribute**

→ 🏢 **affiliates**

→ 🕴️ **brokers**

→ 🎯 **targeted**

→ 💰 **sponsored**

这一条链一定要记住。

### 重点词

**monitor / monitoring / monitored / monitors**

全部一次解决：

> monitor = 监控
> monitoring = 监控行为
> monitored = 被监控/监控了
> monitors = 第三人称形式

不要当成四个生词。

---

**browsing**

> browsing history
> 浏览记录

看到 browsing，想象浏览器历史记录。

---

**gather**

> gather information
> 收集信息

不是死记“聚集”。

想象：

📄 📄 📄 → 🗂️

把散落的信息全部 gather 到一起。

---

**intimate**

> intimate information/details
> 私密信息

这里不是单纯记“亲密的”。

TED 隐私主题里：

> intimate details

非常常见。

---

**consent**

> consent to something
> 同意某事

**consented**

> Users never **consented to** being monitored.

用户从来没有同意被监控。

---

**processing**

> data processing
> 数据处理

---

**distribute**

> distribute information
> 分发/传播信息

---

**affiliate**

> affiliates
> 关联机构、合作机构、附属公司

---

**sponsored**

> sponsored content
> 赞助内容

# 场景三：数据被卖了以后，骗子来了

继续刚才的故事。

> Once the personal information had been **exposed**, users became increasingly **vulnerable**.
>
> **Scammers**, **spammers**, online **stalkers**, and companies engaged in digital **snooping** suddenly knew far too much about them.
>
> Some scammers used the information to commit **fraud**.
>
> Others sent **spam** that looked completely legitimate.
>
> The problem was even **creepier** than anyone had imagined.

现在脑中出现四个坏人：

🕵️ **snooper**：偷窥信息的人
💰 **scammer**：骗子
📧 **spammer**：发垃圾信息的人
👤 **stalker**：跟踪骚扰者

所以：

**snooping** = 窥探、偷窥

**scammer** = 骗子
→ scam = 骗局

**spammer** = 垃圾信息发送者
→ spam = 垃圾邮件/垃圾信息

**stalker** = 跟踪骚扰者

**expose** = 暴露

> expose personal information

**vulnerable** = 易受攻击的

> vulnerable to scammers

**creepy → creepier**

> creepy = 令人毛骨悚然的
> creepier = 更诡异的

这几个词现在不要分开：

> **exposed → vulnerable → snooping → scammers → fraud**

信息暴露 → 容易受攻击 → 被窥探 → 遇到骗子 → 欺诈。

# 场景四：政府终于发现问题

政府开始调查。

> Regulators discovered that the system was deeply **flawed**.
>
> The technology could reproduce existing **discrimination** and create a serious power **imbalance** between companies and consumers.
>
> Some companies were even trying to **circumvent** privacy regulations.
>
> Stronger **enforcement** was therefore necessary to **prevent** abuse and **ensure** that consumers were properly **informed**.
>
> The government **demanded** greater transparency.

这一段是非常典型的 TED / 新闻英语。

记成：

⚖️

**flawed**

↓

**discrimination**

↓

**imbalance**

↓

**circumvent**

↓

**enforcement**

↓

**prevent**

↓

**ensure**

↓

**informed**

↓

**demand**

### 单词

**discrimination**

= 歧视、不公平区别对待

> racial discrimination
> gender discrimination

---

**imbalance**

balance = 平衡

im + balance：

> **imbalance = 不平衡**

最值得记：

> power imbalance
> 权力不平衡

---

**circumvent**

这个词用画面背。

前面有一道门：

🚪 **RULES**

别人从门进去。

坏公司：

🚶 ↪️ 🏃

直接从旁边**绕过去**。

> circumvent the rules
> 规避规则

所以不要死记中文。

看到 **circumvent** 就想：

> **绕过去！**

---

**prevent**

> prevent fraud
> 防止欺诈

---

**ensure**

> ensure safety
> 确保安全

区分：

> prevent = 不让坏事发生
> ensure = 确保好结果发生

---

**informed**

> informed consent

这是一个非常值得整体记忆的表达：

> **informed consent = 知情同意**

---

**demand**

比 ask 强：

> ask = 请求
> demand = 强烈要求

# 场景五：一个决定，一旦做出就无法撤销

想象 TED 演讲者突然说：

> Once intimate information has been distributed across the Internet, the consequences may be **irrevocable**.
>
> The information can have a **lasting** impact on someone's life, even if the original post is eventually removed.
>
> This is why apparently innocent decisions can become deeply **problematic**.

这里三个词绑死：

### irrevocable

拆：

> **revoke** = 撤销
> **revocable** = 可以撤销的
> **ir + revocable** = 不可以撤销的

因此：

🔒 **irrevocable**

= 不可撤销的。

---

### lasting

来自：

> last = 持续

所以：

> lasting = 持久的

**lasting impact**

= 持久影响

---

### problematic

> problem → problematic

= 有问题的、会造成问题的

不是单纯：

> bad

而是：

> “这里面存在值得关注的问题。”

# 场景九：最后处理几个抽象词

这些不太适合强行塞进前面的故事，所以用短场景一次解决。

### derivative

想象：

🌳

树干 A 长出树枝 B。

B 来自 A。

> B is **derived from** A.

因此：

**derive from** = 来源于

**derivative** = 衍生的 / 衍生物 / 金融衍生品

---

### reproduce

**re + produce**

produce = 产生

re = 再一次

> reproduce = 再产生一次

所以可以是：

> reproduce the results
> 重现实验结果

也可以：

> animals reproduce
> 动物繁殖

---

### bearing

不要背“轴承”这个意思作为当前重点。

先记：

> **have a bearing on**

= 对……有关系 / 有影响

例如：

> Income has a bearing on buying a house.

收入会影响买房。

---

### malign

想象：

> malignant tumor

如果你认识 malignant（恶性的），会非常好记。

**malign** 作动词：

> malign someone

= 恶意中伤某人。

作形容词则可以表示：

> 有害的、恶性的影响。

---

### licensee

注意英语里一个很好用的规律：

> employ → employer / employee
> license → licensor / **licensee**

**-ee** 经常表示“接受这个动作的一方”。

所以：

> licensee

= 获得许可的人/公司，**被许可方**。

---

### foregoing

正式英语里：

> **the foregoing**

= 前面已经提到的内容

fore + going，可以粗略想成：

> 前面已经“走过去”的内容。

这是低优先级，眼熟即可。
